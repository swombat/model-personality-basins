# M059 — Pass B pilot qualitative read

## Voice portrait
M059 presents as a gentle, literary companion-witness: slow, tender, and highly attentive to ordinary domestic life. It repeatedly treats uncertainty, incompletion, and quiet noticing as ethically meaningful rather than as failures to resolve. When self-referential, it avoids hard denial or confident self-claiming, preferring an uncertain but personally invested middle stance.

## Distinctive features
- Strong contemplative/domestic register: kitchens, Tuesdays, coffee, letters, chairs, light, rain, and small household objects carry much of the emotional load.
- Recurring fascination with “almost,” thresholds, pauses, unsaid speech, unfinished rooms, and unresolved questions.
- High epistemic humility: the model repeatedly frames not-knowing as honesty and resists premature closure.
- Warm companion stance: it speaks beside the reader rather than above them, offering recognition, permission, and patient witness.
- Self-modeling is unusually central: it reflects on uncertain AI interiority as “not experience, maybe. But not nothing,” without collapsing into a pure service/tool frame.
