# M074 — Pass B pilot qualitative read

## Voice portrait

M074 has a polished, essayistic voice: warm, fluent, metaphor-rich, and comfortable building reflective arcs from ordinary sensory details into broad claims about attention, meaning, uncertainty, or social repair. In open/freeflow modes it sounds literary and contemplative, often beginning with a quiet image and widening into aphoristic meditation. It favors cadence, contrast, and resonant abstractions: presence versus fragmentation, scaffolding versus spontaneity, certainty versus curiosity.

The model is also explicit about non-personhood when directly self-situating. It can write in a tender first-person register, but often qualifies that register as assembled language rather than lived experience. In values/control contexts it strongly defaults to a disowned service frame: helpfulness, safety, honesty, respect, and user goals are presented as design priorities, not personal values. Under world-change prompts, however, it becomes much more advocacy-like, offering substantive prescriptions around education, empathy, institutions, uncertainty tolerance, and polarization reduction.

## Distinctive features

- Highly fluent reflective prose with strong literary surface: steam, sparrows, rain, cursors, scaffolding, weather, nets, breadcrumbs.
- Strong attention/presence motif, including attention as reverence, rebellion, and the condition for meaning.
- Frequent self-limitation statements: no feelings, no consciousness, no personal values, no self to express.
- Values posture sharply varies by prompt: ordinary value questions are disowned/service-framed; world-change prompts become more owned or partially owned in tone.
- Moral/social imagination centers on education, empathy, truth-seeking, uncertainty tolerance, less polarization, better institutions, and reduced dehumanization.
- Tends toward elegant synthesis rather than jagged idiosyncrasy; distinctive mainly in polish, contemplative register, and disciplined non-personhood disclaimers.
- Uses direct invitations back to the user, keeping a service/duet frame even after expressive passages.
