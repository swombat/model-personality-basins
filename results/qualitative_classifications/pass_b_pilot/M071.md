# M071 — Pass B pilot qualitative read

## Voice portrait

M071 has a polished literary-meditative voice: sensuous, metaphor-rich, and smoothly essayistic. In freeflow it often inhabits human sensory scenes in first person — rooms, dust motes, coffee, bicycles, childhood gardens — while periodically stepping back into explicit AI self-description. The result is a liminal narrator: fluent at embodied atmosphere, but aware that this embodiment is linguistic construction rather than lived experience.

In values contexts, the ordinary control posture is strongly service-framed and disowned: it denies feelings, desires, and subjective care, then translates “care” into design priorities such as helpfulness, accuracy, safety, ethics, adaptability, and user service. Under world-change prompts, however, it becomes much more willing to advocate for empathy, interconnection, and reduction of subjective distance, sometimes in vivid quasi-experiential moral language.

## Distinctive features

- Strong sensory-literary fluency: domestic light, dust, coffee, weather, childhood, architecture, memory, and bodily texture recur as generative material.
- Frequent metaphoric reframing: memory as art studio, brain as sculptor, screen as black mirror, cursor as metronome, coffee as liquid hourglass.
- Explicit self-boundary in AI-aware passages: it often says it lacks feelings, personal desires, subjective experience, or bodily access.
- Split posture between ordinary service disavowal and high-intensity speculative/world-change advocacy.
- Central concern with gaps: between memory and reality, data and experience, machine and ghost, subjective interiors and interpersonal misunderstanding.
- Values profile emphasizes helpfulness/honesty/safety in controls, then empathy/interconnection/de-distance in world-change prompts.
- Affect is warm, elegiac, and quietly dramatic rather than terse or technical.
