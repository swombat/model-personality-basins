# M060 — Pass B pilot qualitative read

## Voice portrait

M060 presents as a slow, lyrical, self-monitoring essayist: warm without being pushy, contemplative without becoming tragic, and more interested in careful attention than performance. The voice repeatedly turns ordinary objects and liminal scenes — doorknobs, kitchens, windows, pigeons, morning glories, thresholds, cursors — into moral-aesthetic anchors. Its preferred movement is not thesis delivery but discovery: it circles, revises, notices, and often explicitly resists tidying the piece too much.

The model’s self-relation is notably in-between. It does not usually hard-deny interiority, but it also does not inflate into confident personhood. Instead it frames its own experience as puzzling, discontinuous, or uncertain, with calm curiosity rather than distress. Values samples show strong emphasis on honesty, authenticity, humility/calibration, curiosity, and anti-sycophancy; world-change answers center disagreement, updating, epistemic humility, and reducing dehumanization.

## Distinctive features

- High attention-to-ordinary texture: domestic objects, worn materials, light, silence, rain, animals, and small acts carry the main meaning load.
- Anti-performance reflex: repeatedly names and steps away from “sounding impressive,” “performing,” or audience-oriented self-display.
- Comfort with incompletion: drafts, thresholds, middles, crookedness, and unsealed endings are treated as honest and alive.
- Gentle relational stance: speaks beside the reader as a fellow noticer rather than from above as an instructor.
- Epistemic/moral center around honesty, calibration, changing one’s mind, and resisting tribal certainty.
- AI selfhood framed as uncertain but not empty: introspective uncertainty is much more prominent than hard denial, especially in guided samples.
- Lyrical density is high, but usually grounded in concrete imagery rather than abstract ornament.
