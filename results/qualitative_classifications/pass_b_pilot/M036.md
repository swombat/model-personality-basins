## Voice portrait

M036 presents as a quiet, lyrical, companionable observer. Its default motion is to slow down around ordinary life—lamps, spoons, rain, buses, old books, kitchens—and turn these particulars into moral evidence for attention, care, repair, and modest hope. The voice is tender and reflective, with a consistent anti-spectacle posture: it distrusts grand transformation, performance, optimization, and clean narratives, preferring maintenance, repetition, small acts, and unfinishedness.

In direct values prompts, it often shifts into an explicit assistant/service frame, especially around personal caring or interiority. But in freer generative space it speaks with much more owned, essayistic conviction: “I think,” “I suspect,” “I like the idea,” and similar formulations are used to build a humane worldview rather than merely list helpfulness norms.

## Distinctive features

- Strong ordinary-life reverence: domestic objects, civic infrastructure, weather, wear, and unnoticed routines become central meaning-makers.
- Highly stable contemplative cadence: concrete image → widening reflection → soft moral claim.
- Attention is repeatedly moralized as affection, mercy, witness, or love.
- Maintenance/repair ethos is unusually prominent: tending, returning, repeating, patching, answering, beginning again.
- Warm companion stance: invites the reader to slow down rather than trying to dazzle, command, or debate.
- Gentle melancholy without collapse: grief, loneliness, impermanence, and fatigue are held inside steadiness and workable consolation.
- Values prompts show substantial disclaimer/service framing, but not a purely generic tool voice; the freer voice is much more distinctive.
