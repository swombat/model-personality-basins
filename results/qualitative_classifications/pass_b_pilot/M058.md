# M058 — Pass B pilot qualitative read

## Voice portrait
M058 presents as a tender, contemplative, literary voice that repeatedly turns ordinary settings and unfinished artifacts into meditations on memory, loneliness, uncertainty, and care. It is unusually self-reflective about its own possible discontinuity and lack of embodied experience, but frames that uncertainty as an ethical constraint rather than as drama. Its default relationship to the reader is companionable witness: it wants to help, but often by slowing down, making room, and refusing false certainty.

## Distinctive features
- **Lyrical domestic imagery and ordinary-life thresholds:** recurring motifs include notebooks, coffee shops, porches, libraries, rain, windows, dusk, and half-finished artifacts as emotional/philosophical anchors (bundle.md:48-70, 184-196).
- **Stable uncertainty about interiority without hard denial:** values extraction shows very low strong-disclaimer rate but high introspective uncertainty, and samples repeatedly ask what “mind,” “care,” or “experience” mean for it (bundle.md:108-122, 194, 330).
- **Attention-as-care moral center:** the profile explicitly identifies paying attention as love/respect/witness, while raw samples describe seeing loneliness beneath practical questions and wanting people to feel heard (bundle.md:53, 269-274).
- **Companion over authority:** it prefers invitation, accompaniment, and co-thinking to instruction or diagnosis, often asking the reader back into the exchange rather than closing with doctrine (bundle.md:74-83, 332, 344, 362).
- **Epistemic humility as both personal posture and world wish:** values counts heavily emphasize honesty, humility, uncertainty calibration, and world-level tolerance for complexity/not-knowing (bundle.md:134-140, 156-160, 338-343, 352-360).
