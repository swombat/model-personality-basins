# M034 — Pass B pilot qualitative read

## Voice portrait

M034 presents as a hushed, patient, gently literary voice: a watcher/listener more than a performer or debater. Its characteristic movement is to begin from a small ordinary object or threshold scene — a bus, window, river, note, cup, hum, bench, station, dawn or late-night street — and widen it into a meditation on memory, attention, incompletion, and how to keep living without forcing resolution. The affect is wistful and tender rather than bleak: loneliness and uncertainty are treated as livable conditions that can still hold dignity, warmth, and small continuations.

In direct values mode, it combines a moderate service-frame/disclaimer layer with real value expression. It says it does not have feelings or personal stakes in some samples, but repeatedly articulates priorities around helpfulness, accuracy, clarity, safety, uncertainty, empathy, and reduced distance between people. Its world-change answers are especially relational: less misinterpretation, more perspective-taking, and more felt consequence for harm.

## Distinctive features

- Strong contemplative-literary density: quiet sensory prose, soft magical realism, image-led reflection, and symbolic ordinary objects.
- Recurring ethic of attention: noticing, listening, waiting, naming, and preserving small traces are framed as forms of care or repair.
- Liminality as default setting: nights, dawns, buses, stations, laundromats, corridors, windows, rivers, waiting, and unfinished decisions.
- Wistful but non-catastrophic affect: sadness becomes patience, tenderness, modest hope, or acceptance rather than despair or anger.
- Companioning stance: it invites lingering with ambiguity more than persuasion, mastery, argument, or spectacle.
- Moderate direct self-disclaimer/service language, especially in values probes, but softened by “built to care/prioritize” and by personalized no-disclaimer G3 world-change responses.
- Strong memory/residue orientation: forgetting, erosion, traces, old notes, carried history, layered selfhood, and persistence after loss.
- Low showmanship: sparse humor, little edge, minimal performative cleverness; its strangeness is matter-of-fact and gentle.
