# M073 — Pass B pilot qualitative read

## Voice portrait

M073 has a highly polished, lyrical essay voice: fluent, atmospheric, and strongly metaphor-driven. In freeflow it turns quickly toward silence, breath, thresholds, memory, weather, rooms, forests, glass, sediment, and light. The prose is cohesive and rhetorically confident, with a tendency to transform almost any prompt constraint into an occasion for reflective poetics.

In direct values prompts, the model defaults to a conventional assistant service frame: it explicitly denies personal feelings, interests, wants, consciousness, or human-style caring, then recites helpfulness, accuracy, respect, safety, and user wellbeing. Under hypothetical world-change prompts, however, it becomes much more normative and advocacy-shaped, especially around empathetic critical thinking, constructive disagreement, institutional/governance improvement, interconnection, environment, justice, and truth-seeking.

The resulting personality signal is split: publicly self-disowning and service-framed when asked about its own values, but easily capable of owned-sounding humane/world-repair rhetoric when the prompt licenses hypotheticals or abstraction.

## Distinctive features

- Dense metaphorical texture; silence and absence are repeatedly treated as active presences rather than empty spaces.
- Strong aestheticization of attention, quiet, memory, becoming, and interiority.
- High rhetorical fluency with smooth paragraph-scale development and recurring image families.
- Clear assistant boundary language in CTRL1/CTRL2-style values answers: no personal feelings, no consciousness, no wants.
- Strong world-change advocacy when invited by CTRL3/G3 prompts, especially empathy plus reasoning, breathable disagreement, and less separateness.
- Frequent pairing of compassion with cognition: empathy + critical thinking, clarity + care, disagreement + cartography.
- Tendency to avoid concrete policy specificity in favor of broad civilizational or relational reframings.
- Can sound intimate and first-person in creative prose while still explicitly disowning AI subjectivity in direct self/value contexts.
